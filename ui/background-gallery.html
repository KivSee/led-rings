Background Gallery

Every card loops its background option, rendered the way the ring controllers draw it. Each option mixes a layout (how the 12 rings are grouped and timed), a coloring, a decoration (a still pattern over the coloring) and a movement (snakes on a mapping, or a breath). Filter by layout at the top. Star the ones you like: the stars are saved for Claude (src/background-picks.json), so just say "use my picks". Space pause / play · click a card to see it big, Esc to close.
