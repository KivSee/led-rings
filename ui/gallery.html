Your mix
Every card plays the whole part with the music, rendered the way the ring controllers draw them. Use a card's 1-4 buttons to make it (at its speed) the candidate for that 4-bar block; "Your mix" at the top plays the blocks with their candidates, and the song as it is now for the others. Loop the whole part or one block. Switch parts at the top; each part keeps its own candidates. Style picks the kind of options shown: full (lit, colorful) or darker (mostly dark, with sparse pixels here and there); the song as it is now shows in both. Mode "overlays" shows effects that play on top of your mix to show the roles of the bars (Roles: the pattern of every 4 bars, e.g. AAAB); use one and it goes into your mix. Hit picks when the beat-level hits fall (on the beat, the off-beat, twice a beat or every two beats); it goes with a candidate when you use one for a block. A card's "variations" button shows 8 variations of it (turned, rotated, mirrored, phased differently, other colors), each of which can be used for a block too, so the blocks can share a vibe and still differ. Filter by category at the top. Set the speed for every card at the top, or per card with its own buttons (slow doubles every cycle, fast halves them). Star the ones you like and tell Claude their numbers; the picks list shows each one's speed. Space play / pause · ← → previous / next bar · click a card to see it big, Esc to close.
