Ring orientation and pixel indexes

  • d2 (the installation) is a clock face. d1 ring r (1-12) sits at r:30, and ids go clockwise.
  • Each d1 has its own clock, with local 12 facing the d2 center. d0 ring i (0-11, pixels 12i-12i+11) sits at local (i + 9):30, going clockwise: the wiring starts 9 small rings in, so d0 3 is at 0:30, next to the inner side.
  • Each d0 has its own clock, with local 12 facing its d1 center. Pixel p (0-11) sits at local p:30, going clockwise.
  • Physical pixel index on a d1 ring: i × 12 + p (0-143).
