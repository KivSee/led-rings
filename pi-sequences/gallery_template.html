Pi Effect Combinations

Every section below is cut from a sequence pulled off the Raspberry Pi's sequence service and played with a port of the ring renderer, on the real segment map. A section is a stretch where the same set of long-running effects is active; effects shorter than 1.5 s count as beat accents and are listed dimmed. The category triggers are generated at random in 30 s blocks and exist only on the Pi. Shemesh on the Pi is newer than the committed song file in chorus 1 and party 2.
